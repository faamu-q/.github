<h1 align="center">FaaMU</h1>

<p align="center"><strong>Quantitative intelligence, built for reasoning.</strong></p>

FAAMU develops specialized artificial intelligence for quantitative reasoning,
financial modelling and scientific computing.

Our flagship model, **FAAMU Q**, is designed around rigorous reasoning in areas
including quantitative finance, stochastic modelling, fixed income and
interest-rate derivatives, derivative pricing, risk, time series and
mathematical computing.

### Focus

- Quantitative and mathematical reasoning
- Stochastic modelling and simulation
- Fixed income and interest-rate derivatives
- Pricing, no-arbitrage and measure-change reasoning
- Risk, portfolio and time-series analysis
- Scientific Python and reproducible research workflows
- Model evaluation for technical and quantitative tasks

### FAAMU Q

FAAMU Q is the primary language-model program developed by FAAMU. The model is
being built for technically verifiable reasoning rather than generic financial
text generation.

Initial language support is planned for **English, French and Spanish**.
