# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

FAAMU is committed to a professional, technically rigorous and respectful
environment.

Participants are expected to:

- communicate respectfully;
- critique ideas and methods rather than people;
- document uncertainty and limitations honestly;
- give appropriate credit;
- avoid harassment, discrimination and intimidation;
- protect confidential or restricted information;
- disclose relevant conflicts of interest.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory
conduct, deliberate misrepresentation of results, plagiarism, doxxing,
credential sharing and unauthorized disclosure of private material.

## Scope

This policy applies to FAAMU repositories, discussions, reviews, issue trackers,
collaborative spaces and events associated with the organization.

## Enforcement

Concerns should be reported privately to the maintainers of the relevant
repository or organization. Reports should not be posted publicly when doing so
would expose private or sensitive information.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, restrict participation, close
discussions, reject contributions or revoke access when necessary to protect
the community and the integrity of the work.
